Biography

Crafting Digital Experiences with Intent.

Hi, I'm Mitra Doosti. I am a UI/UX designer with a deep passion for transforming complex human problems into simple, usable, and visually compelling visual products.

My approach blends user psychology, empirical research, and expressive modern visual design. I believe every pixel, color gradient, and layout decision should serve a distinct purpose—reducing cognitive friction while evoking delight.

Over the years, I've led and contributed to projects spanning smart home automation ecosystems, specialized art e-commerce platforms, and high-impact healthcare tools.
